# The diamond, the measure, the 4:3

Scripts: `work_scripts/dm_common.py` (loader), `dm_A1.py` to `dm_A6.py` (diamond), `dm_B1.py` to `dm_B4.py` (measure), `dm_C1.py`, `dm_C2.py` (4:3). Tables: `work_data/diamond_occ.csv`, `diamond_counts_contexts.csv`, `tablet_records_cup_load.csv`. Reading order = stored order reversed. "Complete seals" = 1,237 seal texts of 3+ signs with no break and no lost sign.

## Headline

1. The diamond is a head-count unit: a hand, a man-share, counted like porters. Three diamonds equal three loads on 22 of 22 tablets that carry both, and the count is 3 on 29 of 29 "n diamonds" faces across six trenches.
2. Diamond (861), its twin drawing (817), the wheel (820) and the 060-920 cap are one slot with four fillers. The three count-bearing openers almost never meet in one text (6 co-occurrences against 36.8 expected) and the same owner text turns up under different ones (15 of 20 repeated bodies).
3. The opener belongs to the seal, not the owner: no link to animal, class, site, zone, period or room.
4. Cups come in 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6, never 5: the divisors of 12. One cup stroke is about 9.2 litres by the measured pots. Six cups is the next rung; a dozen is the written block 055.
5. The 4:3 is one office's standard issue, not per head and not a conversion. 21 of the 23 tablets with three loads are one dumped batch from one trench.

### Interchangeable or different

- Same slot: co-occurrence in one complete seal is 1 (861+817), 4 (861+820), 1 (817+820); chance would give 11.9, 13.9, 11.0.
- Same body: 20 owner texts (body after opener + 2) sit on 2 or more seals; 15 of them carry different openers (`415 100 740` under 820, 817, 861, 861; `003 390` under nine seals with 817, 820 and 861). Same-opener pairs 19 against 24.5 by shuffling: no stickiness.
- 861 and 817 are one sign drawn twice: after-count mix on opener seals is identical (chi2 0.0), third-sign mix is within shuffle (JSD 0.41 against 0.35 +- 0.04).
- The wheel is a different counter: after-count mix against 861 chi2 59.2 (p 2e-9), against 817 chi2 56.3 (p 7e-9). It takes 1 and tall 2, and ends a text 35 times against 47 for 861 on a base of the same size. Slot: shared. Counts: wheel is looser (1, 2, 3, 4 and tall 2).
- The opener takes room: opener seals carry 3.4 signs after the opener against 5.1 on seals without one (p 2e-15 for 861, 817, 820, cap) and 0.57 to 0.86 name signs against 1.62.

### Head to head

| reading | what it predicts | what the numbers say |
|---|---|---|
| time unit (day, month, season) | count varies by document; batch mates share it; opener independent of owner | Count does not vary: 2 on 98 percent of seal openers, 3 on 29 of 29 tablets in 6 trenches. Opener independent of owner: yes. Count equals the load count 22 of 22: no reason under time. |
| group of people (gang, household) | opener goes with findspot, class, animal, room | Seals: p 0.12 to 0.96 on every one; same name under different openers. Tablets: yes, count = head-count (22 of 22), short strokes (the living-things series, 93 percent short). |
| place or institution | opener goes with site or zone | V 0.10 to 0.27, p 0.3 to 0.9. A place is not counted three. |
| quantity unit (sack) | count scales with amount | Fixed at 3 while cups run 2 to 4. |

Pick: head-count unit. On tablets "3 diamonds" is the number of hands, written beside "3 loads" (3 carriers). On seals "diamond + 2" is the same unit in a fixed formula, a pair, placed in the opener slot with the wheel and cap as its alternatives; it belongs to the seal's form, not to the owner. The head-count unit explains the two things nothing else does: the 22 of 22 equality and the short-stroke series.

## B. The measure

### Written bands

- Cup (700) counts on tablets: 1 five times, 2 99, 3 145, 4 113, 6 twice, 5 never; additive fours `700 032 032` (H-1879, H-1899) and `033 031 700` (H-2207); pot H-371 `036 700` is a sixth six. Cup counts: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 are the divisors of 12 and cover 99 percent of 388.
- Pots, whole text a bare tall count: 91 pots: 1 eight, 2 41, 3 23, 4 12, 5 six, 6 one. Pots stay in 1 to 4 for 84 of 91 (92 percent); the 7 above are four broken texts and three whole ones. Pots mode is 2 (45 percent); tablet cups mode is 3 to 4. Homogeneity pots against tablets chi2 41.8 (p 4e-9): pots are small consignments, tablets bigger.
- Pots carrying the cup: H-372 `033 700` (3), H-371 `036 700` (6), M-1374 `033 700`, H-2272 `700 033`, H-1546 `700`, M-1588 `700 031`.
- The other container in the same series is the jar 740: tablet counts 1 five, 2 43, 3 four, plus stacked 6 and 7 three and four times.
- Count tokens over the corpus by value (all series): 1 464, 2 1,421, 3 820, 4 286, 5 92, 6 62, 7 87, 8 7. Share on 1, 2, 4, 8 is 67 percent (2 carries it, from the opener); 8 is 7 tokens against 87 for 7. The written counts are not a binary ladder.

### Physical anchor (web)

Wells' measured inscribed pots, as quoted by Rao and others: three long strokes 27.30 litres, six long strokes 55.56, seven 65.89; one long stroke 9.24 litres (9.10, 9.26, 9.41, spread 3.4 percent). So one cup stroke is about 9.2 litres and 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 cups are 18 / 28 / 37 / 55 litres. The weight series (cubical chert, binary 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 then decimal, base near 0.85 g, 16 units about 13.6 g) is a different scale: the written counts do not sit on its steps. The match is with 3-smooth sizes: weights double, vessel counts use 2, 3, 4, 6.

### Ladder (building on it)

- 1 cup = 1 tall stroke = about 9.2 L.
- 2, 3, 4 cups = the working band (consignments).
- 6 cups = the next rung (about 55 L; pots H-371, tablets H-801 and H-1881, `036 700`; written also `033 033` 13 times). Five is never written as cup + 5, 3 + 2 or 4 + 1; the sequence jumps 4 to 6.
- 12 cups = the dozen, written as the single block 055 (four strokes over four over four; 62 tokens, "one 055" on 13 of 16, Lothal tags `705 500 741 001 055 220 740 090`, 11 copies).
- Five itself is carried by sign 171: it takes tall 5 in 17 of 29 and tall 2 + 1 as `171 032 031`. It heads consignment lines (`171 035 415`, `171 033 700 415`, `171 032 031 240 636 740 400`), and both 6-cup tablets carry a 171 face: H-801 `036 700 | 171 035 415`, H-1881 `036 700 | 171 033 700 415`. 171 is a line-head word (issue, total), with the five written beside it, not a cup unit.
- The next rung inside one tablet type is the jar line: `027 032 740 400`, `025 032 740`, `028 032 740 400` (blocks 025 to 029, rows of slanted strokes) beside a cup line of 3 or 4: H-2204 (4), H-2206 (4), H-2207 (3+1), H-2208 (4), H-2209 (3), H-2115 (3). All E-trench 10W/11 steatite. 2 jars is fixed, cups vary.

## C. The 4:3

Multi-face tablets: 787 (736 two-face, 51 three-face); 408 carry a numbered cup.

- Per head: out. Cups with three loads 3.87 (n 23), with a bare carrier 3.18 (n 34), ratio 1.22 against 3 predicted (MWU p 0.0003 for the small rise). Inside trench E-11, three loads gives 4 cups 21 of 21, a single carrier line gives 4 cups 7 of 9 (`176 156 | 034 700`: H-1828 to H-1831, H-1844, H-1870, H-1871).
- Per trench / office: in. Cup count by trench chi2 101.9, 40 dof, p 3e-7. E-11 gives 4 on 42 of 61 (69 percent), F Trench I 3 on 30 of 64, E-54 4 on 13 of 31. Carrier faces predict 4 cups (61 percent with a carrier line against 25 percent without), the load count does not.
- Conversion (4 small = 3 big): out. The cup count stays 4 or 2 or 3 when the diamond count is 2 (seal-style fronts: cup 4 x4, 2 x2, 3 x1) and the diamond is 3 at 3 loads only. The ratio is fixed because the crew is fixed.
- Batch: H-2218 to H-2239 are one dump of 22 steatite prisms, Period 3B secondary deposit outside the perimeter wall, Trench 11, east of Mound E (Kenoyer and Meadow 2010), 21 with `4 cups` and one `3 cups` (H-2238, a different trench). Three-loads-with-cup tablets are 23, so 22 of 23 are this one batch. Face order varies (13 with diamonds first, 7 with cups first) so the prism faces are three lines of one chit, not a sequence.

Read: a tablet is a porter-gang delivery chit. The gang leader held it: 3 hands, 3 loads carried, and the office's standard 4 cups (about 37 L, 12 L a man). Handed in at the Mound E depot the chit was kept by the issuing office, the cups were measured out, and the spent prisms were thrown outside the wall in one batch: 21 identical copies are 21 identical deliveries by a crew of three. The 12 behind the numbers is 4 cups times 3 men.

## Live leads out of this

1. Hands unit: look for `n diamonds` beside `n` of any other person sign (woman 140, staff man 142) in the same face set; prediction equal counts.
2. 6 cups: every cup+6 object (H-371, H-801, H-1881) predicts a 171 face or a pot of 55 L; measure H-371 if the pot survives.
3. Dozen: texts with 055 predict cup counts of 4 or 6 when beside a cup line.
4. Jar lines `NNN 032 740 400` beside E-10W/11 cup faces: the 025 / 027 / 028 block is a quantity of the jar goods; group by block and cup count.
