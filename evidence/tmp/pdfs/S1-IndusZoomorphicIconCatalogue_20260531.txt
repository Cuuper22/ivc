Notes: The focus on instruments of reproduction and mass production makes some exclusions necessary. These are listed below.
Zoomorphism on what CISI calls incised tablets is excluded as the expression can be one-off, ad hoc, and non-standard. The incised “miniature tablets” from the lower levels of Harappa are known to be the earliest examples of the fully developed Indus script, which then give way to the embossed ones that
are often mass-produced in moulds (Shah and Parpola 1987: XVI). Incised tablets are not intended for stamped reproduction as is certainly the case with inscribed seals. Their contents are read straight and not from their mirrored impressions.
Cylinder seals and impressions thereof are excluded, as their total count does not attain two digits (CISI).
The study glosses over related morphisms, anthropo-, phyto- and geometric, and does not feature them in the tabulation unless they occur on an object that features an animal.
