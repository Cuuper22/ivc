# H-2218 Through H-2239 Minimal Contrast Team Review

Date: 2026-05-25

## Purpose

This stores the source, linguistic, and adversarial review of the H-2237/H-2238 minimal-contrast packet.

## Linguistic Review

`H-2237` and `H-2238` are the sharpest internal variants in the H-2218 through H-2239 series because both sit in group 3 and the same local template class, `template_700_861_15x`.

They become usable minimal contrasts only if source validation confirms:

- `H-2237`'s `154` is visually real, not damage, incomplete impression, allography of `156`, or transcription drift.
- `H-2238`'s `033` is visually real, not allography or misreading of `034`.
- All three sides are correctly assigned to physical or source-stable side nodes.
- The shared template order `700|861|15x` is not just catalog or image ordering.
- Group 3 comparability holds across manufacturing batch, object class, side inventory, and source notes.
- The invariant sides remain source-stable.

If source validation succeeds, the admissible claim is narrow:

```text
Inside the H-2218 through H-2239 tablet series, 154/156 and 033/034 behave as contrastive substitutions in fixed side-role slots.
```

That would support paradigmatic contrast inside a bounded tablet codebook. It would not identify the function.

## Adversarial Review

Failure modes:

- overfitting to two memorable singletons;
- group 3 workshop/manufacturing effects;
- Fig. 4 adjacency or publication-order artifact;
- group 3 size/measurement cluster mistaken for a sign function;
- local side order being catalog/editorial rather than physical;
- catalog transcription error in `154/156` or `033/034`;
- public Fig. 4 image too coarse for diagnostic strokes;
- broken/damaged strokes creating the apparent variants;
- copy/workshop template drift;
- HARP/CISI/Fig. 4/local-row mismatch;
- wrongly treating `H-2237` and `H-2238` as one paired contrast even though they vary different slots.

Controls required:

- all seven group-3 objects, not just H-2237/H-2238;
- at least two group-1 and two group-2 normal examples for `+156-003+` and `+700-034+`;
- source-confirmed side labels and physical side positions;
- diagnostic-stroke visibility for `154/156` and `033/034`;
- damage and photo-quality status;
- HARP/CISI/Fig. 4/local-row crosswalk;
- copy/workshop hand notes if available.
