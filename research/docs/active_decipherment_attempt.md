### H1: Small-Tablet Administrative Format Marker

Claim:

`003`, `156`, `861`, and `034` are not values by themselves. They mark a compact tablet format or administrative token class.

Predictions:

- The association should survive when H-2218 through H-2239 are removed, at least for `034`.

- New object-level images should confirm these signs really occur on short sides, not only in catalog ordering.

Kill conditions:

- The signal disappears after removing H-2218 through H-2239 and close duplicate families.
- Source images show side assignments or sign segmentations are wrong.
- The same marks distribute equally across large and small objects after stronger source validation.

### H2: Three-Side Batch/Issue Formula

Claim:

The H-2218 through H-2239 template is a batch formula: one side identifies a series/class, one side carries a controlled short mark, and one side carries a fixed role ending in `003`.

Predictions:

- H-2218 through H-2239 should show consistent physical side roles under real images, even if catalog side order swaps.
- Manufacturing groups should not fully determine sign roles.
- H-2237 and H-2238 variants should sit in predictable positions, not random sides.
- The H-2237/H-2233 pair should preserve the `154/156` difference under source images while holding the other two sides fixed.
- The H-2238/H-2230/H-2233 packet should preserve the `033/034` difference under source images while holding `861-003` and `156-003` fixed.

Kill conditions:

- Plate/source images show the three catalog rows are not distinct physical sides.
- Side roles collapse to publication order or manufacturing group.
- Variants are transcription errors rather than visual sign variants.
- H-2237/H-2233 or H-2238/H-2230 fail object identity, side-role equivalence, direction, or diagnostic-stroke visibility.

### H3: Measurement Tier Marker

Claim:

One or more short-mark tokens encode a measurement tier or administrative class tied to object size.

Predictions:

- Token classes should create monotonic size tiers, not just smaller-vs-larger splits.
- The relation should survive within narrow object families such as Harappa `TAB:I` only.
- If `034` is a tier marker, `032/033/034` should form an ordered or at least contrastive sequence.

Kill conditions:

- No ordinal relation appears among `032`, `033`, `034`.
- Measurement association is fully explained by one object family.
- Thickness and side count refuse to cooperate under source-validated data.

### H4: Nonlinguistic Emblem/Form-Factor Code

Claim:

The marks may be a nonlinguistic administrative code that indexes object form, batch, issuer, or handling class rather than language.

Predictions:

- Strong short-mark structure can exist without phonetic recoverability.
- Known-script scarcity comparators will not rescue semantic reading unless metadata/function is available.
- A synthetic admin-code system should be able to mimic some of the pattern, but not the exact source-anchored H-series variants if those are real.

Kill conditions:

- A linguistic or phonetic model predicts the same side-mark families better across held-out objects without ad hoc sign choices.
- The marks align with independent lexical/onomastic constraints from a credible source.
