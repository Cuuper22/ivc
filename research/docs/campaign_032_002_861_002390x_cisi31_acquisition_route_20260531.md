## Result

`Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions, Volume 3: New material, untraced objects, and collections outside India and Pakistan, Part 1: Mohenjo-daro and Harappa`.

The official/library-style route is strong:

- Harappa identifies it as Vol. 3.1 for Mohenjo-daro and Harappa and gives OCLC `718357051`.
- University of Helsinki confirms the title, editors, publisher, ISBN `978-951-41-1040-5`, year `2010`, and series `Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae, Humaniora 359`.
- Antiquity/Cambridge review metadata confirms extensive plate material: `363` black-and-white plate pages and `48` colour plate pages.
- A secondary Kalyanaraman review says Vol. 3.1 ranges include `m1660` to `m2132` and `h1020` to `h2590`, which would cover both `M-1825` and `H-1993`.
