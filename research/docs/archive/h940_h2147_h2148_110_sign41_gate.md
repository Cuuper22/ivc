# H-940 / H-2147 / H-2148 `110` And Parpola Sign-No.-41 Gate

Date: 2026-05-26

This note is a gate — a checkpoint the evidence must pass before the project may take a further step. The step under review is tempting: Parpola numbers a sign 41, the project's own transcription numbers a token `110`, and three objects seem to carry both. The note exists because the first draft of that argument was an overclaim, and this is where the correction is recorded.

## Question

Does Parpola's sign-no.-41 reverse-side clue now let us identify local Lipi `110`, or does it only create a controlled crosswalk hypothesis? A crosswalk lines up one sign-numbering system against another, sign by sign. Lipi is the project's working transcription corpus.

## Decision

Accepted:

- The checked local Lipi metadata contains six rows with local sign token `110`.
- H-940, H-2147, and H-2148 are the current three-object Parpola sign-no.-41 branch.
- H-2100, H-2152, and the Kanmer row are outside `110` controls that must be included in the next crosswalk test.
- H-940 B is a source-visible local `+110+` panel.
- H-2147 is public object-visible, but its current visual weight is component-level only, not a clean single-sign-panel witness.
- H-2148 is source-visible at object and count-level row mapping through Kenoyer 2005 Figure 14.1.
- The `local 110 <-> Parpola sign no. 41` question remains a P0 crosswalk target, now with mandatory outside controls.

Rejected:

- The claim that local `110` appears only in H-940, H-2147, and H-2148.
- Accepted local `110 = Parpola sign no. 41` mapping.
- Any value, meaning, phonetic reading, language identification, or translation.
- Any use of H-2147's visible lower panel as a clean local `673.1 ]110+` single-sign panel before row-to-panel/source-sign mapping.
- Any use of H-2148 beyond count-level row mapping before higher-resolution/source-side convention notes are acquired.
- Any use of H-2100, H-2152, or Kanmer `4881.1` before source panels are acquired or route-checked.
