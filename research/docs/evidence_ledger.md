## Current Evidence Limits

- We do not yet have a local authoritative IVC corpus.
- We have source-visible object or panel bindings for some targets, but no accepted image-level numeric sign mapping, segmentation, value, or translation-grade validation.
- We do not yet have ICIT access.
- We do not yet have an accepted sign-list crosswalk; only provisional positional candidates exist.
- We do not yet have reproducible structural results on an authoritative, image-validated corpus.
- Therefore no translation claim is currently admissible.
