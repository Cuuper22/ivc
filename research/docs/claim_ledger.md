# Claim Ledger

Date: 2026-10-01

This ledger is the project's scoreboard. It records what has been accepted as a finding, what is still being tested, and what has been retracted. A claim enters the accepted counts only after two adversaries have had their turn: the forger, a battery of null models and matched negative controls that measures how often the claimed pattern appears without the claimed cause, and the skeptic, a hostile review that hunts for unresolved fatal objections. Until both pass, a result stays a candidate, no matter how tempting it looks.

The authoritative machine-readable ledger is `data/claim_ledger/claims.json`. This document is its human-readable companion.

Reading direction: the Lipi catalogue stores inscriptions in the reverse of the conventional (Mahadevan) reading order, so every Lipi-based "terminal", "tail" or "closure" in this ledger means stored-final, which is the reading-initial end under the conventional reading. Evidence and the list of affected claims: `reading_direction_note.md`. Physical writing direction per object is not established.

## Accepted Structural Finding

The ledger keeps one accepted structural item. Since the 2026-07-12 checkpoint (`replacement_run_checkpoint_20260712.md`) it is a descriptive observation about two artifacts, not an inferential result.

Claim ID: `accepted_struct_002_861_533_717_restricted_tail_2026_05_29` (the ID predates the current wording and is kept for stable references).

Accepted wording: M-376 and M-391 are two source-visible attestations of the same three-position terminal string `861 | 533 | 717` after `002`. Terminal here means stored-final in the Lipi catalogue, which is reading-initial under the conventional reading. Sign `533` occurs only on these two seals in the 5,679-row Lipi source. This is not a rule. The inference that `533-717` is a source-independent fixed two-sign unit is demoted below acceptance, and the `533` dependency on `717` is sensitive to how sign families are cut (Parpola group 222 and Mayig P228 include forms, such as those on M-17 and M-26, that occur without `717`).

False-positive rates: the earlier record (`0.0002`, `0.001`, `0.006`) was computed for the prefix `002-861`, which was chosen after looking at the data. Those numbers are not valid false-positive rates and the accepted statement does not rely on them. A post-hoc prefix search finds many comparable repeated terminal cells (96 in strict raw rows and 40 after exact dedup in the earlier skeptic pass).

Not accepted: exact source-normalized `861/533/717` token boundaries, any fixed-unit or rule reading, sign value, sign meaning, phonetics, language family, external anchor, or translation.
