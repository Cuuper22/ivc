Date: 2026-05-26

## Question

What does Parpola 2019 mean by "sign no. 41" in the H-940 / H-2147 / H-2148 reverse cluster, and does that source the H-2148 local `110` branch?

## Result

Parpola 2019's numbering convention is now sourced: the paper says it uses Parpola 1994, pp. 70-78, Fig. 5.1 as the sign list throughout. In the downloaded Parpola 1994 PDF, Fig. 5.1 begins on printed p. 70 / PDF page 88, and sign no. 41 is visible on that page. Its variants include a solid kneeling/person-with-raised-object form matching Parpola 2019's prose description of "a [kneeling] man holding a pot."

That resolves the article-convention gate. It does not solve the local crosswalk.
