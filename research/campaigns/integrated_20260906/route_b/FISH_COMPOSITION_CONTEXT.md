The repeated-fish rival `267–99–59–59–211` is Harappa seal 4232, raw picture 31. The four forms in the `267–99` construction therefore come from four different cities. That reach is real, but there is no within-city contrast for this complete chain. Repeated objects are not independent evidence for a new operation.

The two original roof-87 frames are perfectly separated by copper versus non-copper medium. Their initial fit alone cannot distinguish a graphical operation from an artifact-specific convention. The new roof-on-marked and joint-base-70 examples refute **“explicit roof-87 only occurs on copper”**. However, no exact roof-only contrast holds both site and medium fixed.

The tick contrast 1177/3103 holds city, medium, direction and raw picture fixed. The joint base-70 contrast 1380/2452 holds city, medium and direction fixed. Those coarse context variables cannot determine the respective fused versus expanded forms. Neither pair controls documented workshop membership or stratigraphic phase. The second pair also fails a naive prediction that normalization must preserve the raw picture code.

The rival roof-102 interpretation's two original exact frames were included in the executed stratification. Neither has a matched city-and-medium pair, so those examples do not rescue a context-independent roof value or supply a special contextual advantage over 87.

Result: retain the composition as a writing-operation candidate with cross-context reach. Do not promote it to synonymy, a shared depicted referent, arithmetic multiplication or a general grammatical feature. The discriminating next evidence would be an already-held same-locality/phase exact contrast or a companion-field prediction where the operation and a production convention disagree.
