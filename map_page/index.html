Tap any line

The Indus script is a set of about 700 signs from 4,000 years ago that nobody can read. It survives on seals (small carved stone stamps: an animal plus a short row of signs) and tablets (tiny tokens with signs on two or three faces). Start with the five blocks below: one real seal, piece by piece. Then tap any line in the tree.
