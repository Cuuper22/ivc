What this is

The Indus script is a set of about 700 signs from 4,000 years ago that nobody can read. It survives on seals (small carved stone stamps) and tablets (tiny clay tokens). The top row is the groundwork; every box below was built on the boxes its lines come from. Tap one to light up everything it rests on and everything it opened.
