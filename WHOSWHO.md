# Who's Who of the Indus Cities

Note (2026-11-17): the English and Dravidian words here are working labels for each sign's job; two decoy tests (jobs/out/decoy_test.md) showed the exact words are not established. See FRAME.md for what is measured.

People and families of Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Lothal, Dholavira and the other Indus cities (about 2600 to 1800 BC), read from their own seals.

Every statement is marked `measured` (a number came out of the catalogue data and can be rerun) or `guess` (a reading we act on). Numbers, tests and scripts: `jobs/out/registry.md`; the tables are `work_data/registry/people.csv` (one row per person) and `houses.csv` (one row per family).

## Key

- **The objects.** Indus people owned small square stamp seals, cut with a few signs and usually an animal. They also left clay tablets, copper tablets, clay tags pressed over cords and jar mouths, and marked pots. Each object has a catalogue id: M- = Mohenjo-daro, H- = Harappa, L- = Lothal, C- = Chanhu-daro, Ns- = Nausharo; "Lipi-n" = the Lipi catalogue's number where no other id exists.
- **How a seal reads.** [opener] [name] [ending]. The **opener** is an optional word in front: "two shares", "a quarter", or an office line. The **name** has up to three parts: the **crest** (the family sign, always first), a **personal part**, and a **tag**. The **ending** says what the person is: "house of" (an owner, the jar sign *kuṭi*), "X's man" (*kuṭi āḷ*, the manager of house X), "chief" (*vēḷ*), "keeper" (*kā*), "junior" (*ciṟ*), "gate-guard" (*vāy kāval*), "standard-bearer" (*koṭi*).
- **Houses.** A house is everyone whose name starts with one crest. Many crests are fish signs, read *mīn* "star" (Bamboo-star, High-star, Dark-star ...): the star houses are the great families. Names were inherited: the same full name turns up across six centuries at the rate time-blind chance gives, and keeps its rank (`measured`, `jobs/out/history.md`), so a "person" below is a holder of a hereditary name.
- **The sounds** are working early-Dravidian (old Tamil family) words, one per sign. The English follows from them. Where a sign has no word yet it shows as [number].
- **Findspots.** Mohenjo-daro was dug in named areas (DK, HR, VS ...) split into numbered buildings: "DKG (S) 7II" = area DK-G south, block 7, house II. Harappa areas are mounds and trenches (F = the granary mound, AB = the citadel, E = the southern mound).
- **The animal** on a seal is not the family: the stand pattern under the unicorn marks the household, and the short-horned bull marks a stone cut for overseas trade (`measured`, `jobs/out/animals.md`).

## The twenty great houses

Ranked by the number of objects that name them (seals, tablets, copper tablets, tags, pots), among houses with at least five seals. All twenty are found at both Mohenjo-daro and Harappa and in all three time periods (`measured`).

**1. High-star (*mīmīn*, crest 240).** 119 objects in 7 cities: Harappa 69, Mohenjo-daro 43, Chanhu-daro, Lothal, Desalpur, Dholavira (`measured`). At Mohenjo-daro it lived in DK-G south (15 objects) and its members share buildings 6 times against 1.6 by chance (P 0.02), in DKG(S) 1I, 8A and 7II (`measured`). At Harappa its name is on 64 tablets, many in F Trench I by the granary (10), F (8) and E Trench 54 (7) (`measured`). Ranks: house of 19, chief 7, manager 3, throne 2 (`measured`). It kept three managers: M-1746 and M-199 (High-star-Branch-Elder's managers, both in houses with High-star owners) and H-440 (`measured`). Its tablets carry the cup measure and the grain words *nel* and *kōṭṭai* (`measured`). Two of its seals bear the overseas bull. A grain-issuing great house with its seat in DK-G (`guess`).

**2. Bamboo-star (*vēymīn*, 235; the star Punarvasu).** 100 objects in 8 cities, the most seals of any house (61 in the registry; Mohenjo-daro 54 objects, Harappa 34, Lothal 5) (`measured`). Its centre is DK-G south (14) and DK (9), and its members pair up in the same building 8 times against 3.8 (P 0.07), above all DKE Trench E (4 seals) (`measured`). Its rank is the gate: 8 gate-guards (*vāy kāval*), more than any other house, plus 7 chiefs (`measured`); L-9 and L-51 are Bamboo-star gate-guards at Lothal. Two managers: M-865 (found beside three Bamboo-star owners in DKE Trench E) and M-360 (`measured`). Three overseas-bull seals. The house that held the city gates (`guess`).

**3. Tax (*iṟai*, 176).** 88 objects, 60 at Harappa, 25 of them in F Trench I beside the granary (`measured`). 55 of its objects are tablets, mostly cup-count chits issued in the name "Tax: of the house, junior" (`measured`). Members share buildings 3 times against 0.7 (P 0.05) (`measured`). One manager (M-15). The granary accounts office (`guess`; `jobs/out/masters.md` called it the seated-person office).

**4. Kalam (*kalam*, the grain measure; 705/706).** 69 objects in 7 cities: Mohenjo-daro 36, Lothal 13, Harappa 12, Dholavira 4, Lakhanjo-daro, Chandigarh, Khirsara (`measured`). It is the Lothal warehouse house: 15 tags, 8 in the warehouse trench SRG 3GX 10, among them the eleven "one dozen fish" tags of its manager (`measured`). Its name sits beside store and measure words on 26% of its objects against 12.7% for other houses (P 6e-7) (`measured`). Ranks: house of 12, chief 7 (the "three-kalam chief" `705-033-520`), keeper 3 (`measured`). Its manager M-100 was found in DKE Trench E with the house's Boxed-cotton seals M-431 and M-432 (`measured`). Harappa seal H-1076 names the "Kalam-Umbrella house", the name on the Dholavira signboard (`measured` placement, `guess` reading).

**5. Home (*il*, 803).** 63 objects in 9 cities, the widest reach of the star-free houses: Mohenjo-daro 35, Harappa 19, Chanhu-daro, Dholavira, Banawali, Kalibangan, Lothal, Rakhigarhi, Nuhato (`measured`). Ten chiefs (more than any house) and 18 owners; no managers (`measured`). Four overseas-bull seals. C-67 and C-4 at Chanhu-daro are its chiefs (`measured`). Its name is joined to Chest by *kiḷai* "kindred" on 11 Harappa tablets (H-278 to H-283 ...) (`measured`). A house of chiefs (`guess`).

**6. Bounty (*viḷai* "produce", 806).** 61 objects in 3 cities (Mohenjo-daro 41, Harappa 19, Dholavira) (`measured`). 21 copper tablets, among them the 14 "Bounty-Sheaf ... share ... month" tablets in 6 areas of Mohenjo-daro (`measured`). Its name stands with grain words on 17% of objects against 5.7% (P 1.4e-5) (`measured`). Rising through time (3 → 11 → 18 objects by period, `history.md`). Chiefs 5. The produce house that recorded crop in copper (`guess`).

**7. Hill (*malai*, 503).** 60 objects, 47 at Mohenjo-daro (`measured`). 32 copper tablets, mostly the 29 identical "of the house of the heir of Hill-Field" (`503-615-752-740`) tablets found in 8 areas of the city (`measured`). 13 owner seals, no managers. The landholding house of Hill-Field (`guess`).

**8. Branch (*kavar*, 415).** 56 objects in 10 cities, the widest reach of all: Harappa 24, Mohenjo-daro 24, and one each at Altyn Depe (Central Asia), Ur (Mesopotamia), Bala-kot, Chanhu-daro, Lothal, Rupar, Dholavira, Karanpura (`measured`). At Altyn Depe the seal reads "Branch-Cotton" (Alt-90). Chiefs 6; the Branch-Star chiefs (`415-220-520`) run through all three periods (`history.md`). Branch is also the personal part of High-star-Branch-Elder, whose managers lived in High-star houses (`measured`). A trading line with far branches (`guess`).

**9. Star (*mīn*, plain fish 220 with no number).** 56 objects in 7 cities, centred on DK-G south (11) (`measured`). Chiefs 5, keepers 2. Tablets with the cup count (11) (`measured`).

**10. Lady (*ammai*, 140).** 50 objects, 42 of them seals (`measured`). Mohenjo-daro DK-G south 13, HR-B 4; DKG(S) 4 and 12V hold two each. Two managers: M-24 ("Manager of the Lady-[906]-Two-Bamboo-star-High-star house", SD 8, early) and M-1165 (`measured`). A seal house, not a record house (`guess`).

**11. Chief (*mutal*, 255).** 45 objects, 24 tablets, Harappa 22 and Mohenjo-daro 18, plus 5 other cities (`measured`). Its name stands with chest, kalam and paddy-field words (`measured`). The "Chief-Staff-Ally" name `255-435-690` is on 34 objects (`masters.md`). An office house of land and grain (`guess`).

**12. Bright-star (*vāḷai*, the scabbard-fish, 233).** 44 objects; 29 seals; 18 owners, 5 chiefs, 2 managers (L-41 at Lothal, M-230), 1 guard, 1 gate-guard (`measured`). Four overseas-bull seals (`measured`). M-88, "Bright-star-King-Guard", DKB 2, early period (`measured`).

**13. Descendant (*maru-*, 798; the Maru clan of the signboard).** 40 objects, Mohenjo-daro 21, Harappa 16 (`measured`). Two "thrones" (M-1989 in DKA 4: "Throne of the Descendant seven great measures house") (`measured`). The Dholavira signboard ends with *maru-* (`guess`: the Maru kin of the signboard).

**14. Peer (*iṇai*, 840).** 37 objects, Harappa 17, Mohenjo-daro 15 (`measured`). The Peer-Two name `840-032` is on 40 objects in 6 cities (`masters.md`); its manager M-1224 (LB 9) carries the multi-headed beast (`measured`). Mohenjo-daro M-56 writes "Peer-Umbrella" (`measured`).

**15. First-star (*orumīn*, 231).** 34 objects, 10 of them copper tablets; chiefs 4; 1 object in the early period, 9 middle, 8 late (`measured`). H-459 at Harappa F Trench I is a First-star gate-guard (`measured`).

**16. Quarter (*kāl*, 061).** 33 objects; 24 seals; owners 15, keepers 4, managers 3 (H-565, M-265, M-947) (`measured`). The house of the **Quarter-King**: "of the Quarter-King-Junior house" (`061-142-400-740`) at Harappa (H-1679, H-451, H-780) and Mohenjo-daro (M-879) (`measured`). Its manager M-265 lived in DKG(S) 9VIII beside the Quarter owner M-408 (`measured`). The royal house of a city quarter (`guess`).

**17. Moon (*tiṅkaḷ*, 900).** 29 objects in 6 cities; 13 owners; one manager (M-194) (`measured`). H-1045, Harappa F Trench VI: "two quarters: of the Five-Moon house" (`measured`).

**18. Dark-star (*iru-mīn*, two tall strokes + fish; Saturn, or Venus).** 25 objects in 6 cities; 10 owners, 2 chiefs, one manager (H-502, Harappa AB), one warrior (M-1763) (`measured`). Its room-mates at Mohenjo-daro share the star word (`star_names.md`).

**19. Chest (*peṭṭi*, 590).** 24 objects in 6 cities (and Boxed-cotton below). Its name stands with store and measure words on 17% of objects against 12.8% (P 0.06) (`measured`). Joined by "kindred" to Home on 11 Harappa tablets (`measured`). M-960 (VSA 2XIII, early): "High-star of the Great-measure-Chest house; chief" (`measured`).

**20. Gate (*vāy*, 550).** 24 objects; ranks: gate-guard 4, owner 3, plain name 6 (`measured`). Two overseas-bull seals. The gate-keeping family, beside the Bamboo-star gate-guards (`guess`).

**And: Boxed-cotton (*peṭṭi-parutti*, 590-390).** 22 objects with a closed ending in 6 cities (71 objects carrying the pair in any position in 9 cities, among them Kish in Mesopotamia and Gonur Depe in Central Asia, `masters.md`) (`measured`). Earliest at Harappa H-1692 (E Trench 11W, early period: "two shares: of the Boxed-cotton house") and H-75 (F Granary: "Junior of the Boxed-cotton house") (`measured`). The cotton-bale trading house (`guess`, from `cotton_house.md`; its own objects here carry cotton words no more than others, 7% against 5.5%).

## The offices

Counts are people in the registry (seals read whole or nearly whole) and, after the slash, objects of every kind (`measured`; full table in `jobs/out/registry.md`).

- **Chief** (*vēḷ*, the spear): 114 / 222, in 13 cities, held by 49 houses; Home, Kalam, High-star and Bamboo-star lead. The ordinary rank of a great-house man (`guess`).
- **Manager, "X's man"** (*kuṭi āḷ*): 41 / 96. The manager lives in his master's house: 5 of 24 at Mohenjo-daro were found in a building with an owner of the master's crest, 2.4 times chance (P 0.046), and their seals are bigger than the owners' (718 against 581 mm2, P 0.016) (`measured`).
- **Keeper** (*kā*): 62 / 100. **Junior** (*ciṟ*): 45 / 349, of which 280 Harappa tablets: the junior writes the chits.
- **Gate-guard** (*vāy kāval*): 25 / 42; Bamboo-star 8, Gate 4. **Guard**: 6 / 12. **Standard-bearer**: 15 / 26. **Throne** (jar + *kaṭṭil*): 10 / 9. **Deputy** (*tuṇai*): 1 / 2. **Warrior** (*maṟavaṉ*): 8 / 11.
- **King** (*kō*): 28 / 39 at Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Lothal. There is no one king house per city: Mohenjo-daro's 22 king objects carry 13 different crests (`measured`). The kings are **Quarter-Kings**: the quarter word stands right before "king" 11 times in 81 against 1.4 by chance (P 1.3e-7) (`measured`), *kāl kō*, a king for each quarter of a city (`guess`). The common royal line is "King's servant" (*kō ūḻiyaṉ*, `142-617`): 25 / 39.
- **Royal** (*aracu*): 30 / 66. **Elder** (*mutu*): 53 / 100. **Tax** (*iṟai*): 51 / 175.
- **Wards**: first ward 87 / 160 in 12 cities; second 23 / 27; third 12 / 22. **Assembly** (*kūṭṭam*): 29 / 48. **Bondsman** (*aṭimai*): 10 / 22. **Cultivator** (*vēḷāḷaṉ*): 0 seals, 8 goods objects (`measured`).
- **The seven-city office** (the opening `920-060-741`): 21 / 27 in 7 cities, held by people of 19 different houses: an office of the whole civilisation open to many families (`measured` counts, `guess` reading).
- **The Dholavira signboard's name**, the Umbrella (*kuṭai*): 6 uses. The signboard and Lothal L-116 both write "quarter: Umbrella" (P 0.004 for the quarter word before it); at Harappa it is the "Kalam-Umbrella house" (H-1076) and a Field name (H-3, F Granary); at Mohenjo-daro "Peer-Umbrella" (M-56) (`measured`). The signboard reads "the quarter of King Umbrella" (`guess`): a Quarter-King of Dholavira, with a namesake line in the Kalam house.
