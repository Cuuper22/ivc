## 3. What went against, and the rescue tried

Every value whose bold prediction (A) did not come true got a better test of the same word (B); every one whose B went against got a new word (C). Numbers are on new texts unless marked Lipi.

- **595 *peṭṭi* "chest"**: counted with short strokes 19 to tall 1 (a chest takes tall ones). B came true (copper tablets and tools 12 of 27, x9.9) and C's new word *paṭṭaṟai* "forge" came true (4 against 1.4). Building on *paṭṭaṟai*; the short counts are the observation that splits the two words, and they side with the forge.
- **440 *koḷ* "received" on pots**: 1 of 19 against 7%. B: a Harappa store word, 10 of 19 at Harappa against 34% (P 0.07). Building on. The Lipi pot excess (12 small Harappa pots whose whole text is 440) is a Harappa habit that the second catalogue does not hold.
- **630 *pal* "many"**: objects with it carry fewer numbers than chance (13 against 20.2). B: it stands in the frames of 632, 6 against 2 (P 0.10). Building on as the same word as 632 and 636; "many" sits in a name, not beside counts.
- **790 and 832, the eye signs, as *kaṉ* "copper"**: 790 on new-text copper 0 of 20; 832 1 of 23. The copper excess of the eye family is carried by 850 (new texts 2 of 13, x3.4; both catalogues 20 of 69, x6.5). 790 stays under test (off seals 8 of 20 against 55%, direction). 832 got a new word, *maṇi* "gem, bead" (the eye with a pupil, *kaṇ-maṇi*): 0 of 23 at the bead towns. 832 is paused; lead: it follows the two-crescent sign 904 and comes before the chief sign 255.

- **877 *nālkāṇi* "four shares" as a big seal**: x0.93 at equal sign count. B: it opens the text 12 of 26 against 29% (Lipi part seen). Under test.
- **240 *mīmīn* "High-star" at Harappa**: 18 of 60 against 34% on new texts. B: names with it take the spear 2 of 9 against 10% (direction). Under test.
- **711 *vaḷḷam* "grain measure" in E trench 11**: 2 of 21 against 10%. B: the cup on the same object 12 of 21 against 2.5, a fact seen in the tablet texts. Under test.
- **167**: *poli* "threshing-floor yield": Harappa mound F 3 of 9 against 34%; copies 10 of 15 against 69%. New word *kā* "shoulder pole": carrier frames 1 against 2. Paused; it ends only `705/706-167` tablets.

## 4. What it opens next

- **The ration system.** *paṭi tiṅkaḷ* "monthly allowance" (320-920) and the cup *nāḻi* make a ration vocabulary. Next: every text with 920 after a goods or measure word read as a monthly issue; the count after the phrase should follow cup sizes (never five).
- **The copper tablets as land records.** Hill, field, sheaves, quarter and lot are all copper words. Next: read all 162 copper tablets of the second catalogue as [house] [land word] [produce] [fraction] and check whether the reverse animal tracks the house.
- **The ship.** *kalam pāy* at Lothal. Next: 705-033 (the commonest 705 pair, "three ships"?) on new texts against port and river sites; and Mesopotamian "Meluhha ship" sources for a loan of *kalam*.
- **The forge.** 595 with 391 and the eye signs. Next: the copper tool texts and copper tablets read as smithy records; 821 is the next sign to give a word (it closes the tool text after the weight).
