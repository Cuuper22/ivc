# Sound list, third version: the object-kind anchor

Scripts (in `work_scripts/`, run in this order): `sound_v3_common.py` (the second-pass loader plus the object kind of every text in both catalogues), `sound_v3_a.py` (one bold prediction per value), `sound_v3_b.py` (a better test of the same word for each that did not come true), `sound_v3_c.py` (a new word for each that still went against), `sound_v3_d.py` (the ship lead), `sound_v3_values.py` (the sorted list, `work_data/sound_v3/values_v3.csv`), `sound_v3_cands.py` and `sound_v3_readings.py` (the 20 new readings, appended to `READINGS.md`), `sound_v3_coverage.py`, `sound_v3_report.py` (this file). Tables: `work_data/sound_v3/`.

Plain words used below:
- **Object kind**: what the text is written on. seal; tag (clay tags and sealings pressed onto goods); pot; tool (copper or bronze implement); bangle; rod (ivory or bone rod); copper tablet; miniature tablet (small moulded or incised tablets).
- **New texts**: the 1,183 lines of the second catalogue (Mahadevan 1977) that have no partner in the first catalogue (Lipi). They were never used to choose any value, so a prediction that comes true there is a prediction in the full sense. In the second catalogue "sealing" stands for tag, "pottery graffito" for pot, "bronze implement" for tool.
- **x4.0** = four times what chance gives. **P** = how often chance alone would give a result at least this strong (P 0.01 = one time in a hundred). Small P means the result points to the word being right; P near 1 means the data points the other way.
- **Count before / after**: a number sign (short or tall strokes) right before or right after the sign. **Tall strokes** are the container count series, **short strokes** the series for other things.
- **Name template**: [holding or office] [name] [ending]. The jar 740 *kuṭi* "house of" closes most names; the spear, carrier and comb stand in its place.
- **DEDR** = Burrow and Emeneau's Dravidian Etymological Dictionary, entry number (local copy in `work_data/lexicons/dedr/`). Words are Old Tamil forms; the meaning column is a guess built on a measured slot.
- "yes" = the predicted direction with P at or under 0.1. "seen" = the tested fact was visible in the Lipi sign profiles printed while choosing, so the value stays under test even when it came true.

## 1. What was found

1. **22 of the 37 values under test now stand on a prediction that came true, and 9 brand-new words came true on data nobody had looked at.** The list is now 58 building on, 17 testing, 19 paused (94 values; it was 34 / 37 / 18 of 89). Every prediction was written into its script before the run.
2. **Sign 320 (a ladder) is *paṭi*, the ration measure.** Tamil *paṭi* is both "rung of a ladder" (DEDR 3850) and "the ordinary measure of capacity; fixed daily allowance for food" (3849). The prediction: if 320 is the allowance, it must be followed by the month sign 920, *paṭi tiṅkaḷ* "the monthly allowance". On new texts 920 follows 320 in 5 of 7 uses, against 0.7% for all signs (P 3e-10). This points hard at the reading: a picture-pun chosen from the dictionary predicted its own neighbour on unseen texts. It opens the ration system: pot M-1601 now reads `320-920-740` *paṭi tiṅkaḷ kuṭi* "monthly allowance, of the house" (READINGS 51).
3. **The copper tablets are field and produce records.** Each land word predicted copper tablets on new texts and got them: *malai* "hill" (503) 3 of 19, x4.0; *vayal* "field" (615) 16 of 86, x4.7, P 3e-7; the new word *cūṭu* "bundle of sheaves" (845) 3 of 9, x8.4; *kāl* "quarter" (061) takes a count before it, 4 of 10 against 15.8%. The text on 29 copper tablets, `503-615-752-740`, reads *malai vayal maka kuṭi* "of the house of the heir of Hill-Field" (READINGS 41), and `798-240-845-061-407` on 6 copper tablets reads "descendant of the High-star house: sheaf-bundles, a quarter, the lot" (READINGS 43). A copper tablet is a land-share record of a house.
4. **Sign 595 is the forge, not a second chest.** If 595 were *peṭṭi* "chest" like 590, it would be counted with tall strokes. On new texts it is counted with short strokes 19 times to tall 1 (against 35% tall for all counts): that went against the chest. Its better test came true the other way: on new texts 595 sits on copper tablets and bronze tools 12 of 27 times, x9.9, P 6e-10. The new word *paṭṭaṟai* "anvil, smithy, forge" (DEDR 3865) predicted it would share a face with the smith, the copper eyes or the seed weight: 4 against 1.4 expected (P 0.04). The copper tool text `391-595-850-480-001-821` (3 tools at Mohenjo-daro) now reads *kollaṉ paṭṭaṟai kaṉ kuṉṟi oru* [821] "smith, forge: copper, one seed-weight, [821]" (391 and 850 under test, 821 unread). 590 keeps *peṭṭi*: its tall counts stand.
5. **Sign 500 is *pāy* "sail" (DEDR 4088), and it brought the ship with it.** The tall triangle on a divider is a sail on a mast. Prediction: a ship word sits at the port. On new texts 500 is at Lothal 8 of 12 times against 8% for all faces (P 4e-7). The sign that stands before it on the Lothal tags, 705 (a U-vessel with a stick standing in it), is then a hull with its mast, *kalam* "vessel, ship" (DEDR 1305, *kalavar* "navigators"). On new texts 500 follows 705 3 of 64 times against 0.4% (P 0.002). The 11 Lothal tags `705-500-741-001-055-220-740-090` read *kalam pāy orukuṭi oru paṉṉiru mīn kuṭi āḷ*, "by ship, first ward: one dozen fish; the house's agent" (READINGS 42). A value made for one sign explained the picture of its neighbour, which had been paused.
6. **Tags speak the language of trade.** *koṭu* "given" (890; the bow without its arrow puns *koṭu* "bent", DEDR 2053/2054) sits on new-text sealings 7 of 9 against 22% (P 0.0006). *vil* "sale" (892) on sealings 12 of 27 (x2.0). *paṉṉiru* "dozen" (055) on sealings 14 of 31 (x2.1) and the fish follows it 4 of 40 against 3.1%. The 8 Kalibangan tags `091-056-002-417-890-892` read "the Pair: [056], two pole-loads, given for sale" (417 *kā* "carrying pole" under test, 056 unread).
7. **Grain measures stack the way measures do.** *kōṭṭai* "great grain measure" (575) takes a count before it on new texts 6 of 14 against 15.8%; *niṟai* "full weight" (171) takes one after it 3 of 7 against 14.1%; the drum 540 is *paṟai*, a rice measure as well as a drum (DEDR 4032), and sits on new-text pots 2 of 7, x4.0; *kuṉṟi* (480) sits on new-text pots 2 of 6, x4.6. Sign 335 is *ūr* "town": the jar or *maka* follows it on new texts 3 of 6 against 11.2%, so `575-335` is *kōṭṭai ūr* "Fort-town". The 10 Harappa tablets `413-575-335-711-033-700` read "grain of Fort-town: measure, three cups" (READINGS 57), and their sister text `413-575-335-740-032-700` reads "grain, of the Fort-town house: two cups" (58): the same place word lands in the possessive slot, as a town name should.
8. **House and kin words keep their slots on unseen texts.** *maka* "child of" (760) is followed by the jar 25 of 29 times (P 3e-21); *piṇai* "allied" (690) 10 of 20; *il* "house" (803) is followed by a fish crest 6 of 22 against 7.8%; *ammai* "lady" (140) opens the text 11 of 17 against 29%; *iṇai* "pair" (840) is followed by two tall strokes 7 of 16 against 1.7% (P 5e-9). *ūḻiyaṉ* "king's servant" (617) carries the jar 13 of 75 against 33%: a title standing where the possessive would be.
9. **The share of the corpus that reads went up by half.** Complete texts in which every sign has a value that is building on: 317 distinct texts (955 faces) under the second list, 477 distinct texts (1,222 of 3,600 faces) now. On goods objects (everything but seals): 715 faces to 858 of 2,060. Counting values under test as well: 674 distinct texts, 1,561 faces.
10. **Twenty more inscriptions read whole**, all on goods objects or goods tablets (READINGS 41-60): copper deeds, the Lothal ship tag, pots with measures and the monthly allowance, an ivory rod, a bangle, grain sale chits issued in 11 copies (`413-892-824` *nel vil vilai* "grain: sale, price"), and receipts from the Harappa granary area.
